About CanineMatch

Many prospective dog owners adopt without understanding whether a specific breed matches their home environment, schedule, and activity level. CanineMatch uses data-backed compatibility scoring to reduce shelter surrenders and ensure long-term adoption success.

Built using real-world records from the Austin Animal Center cross-referenced with AKC behavioral benchmarks for over 270 breeds.
